Privacy Policy & Medical Data Protection

At NgipinSolution Biñan and ClinicFlow, we recognize the deeply personal nature of dental and medical health records. We are committed to upholding your fundamental right to privacy in full compliance with Republic Act No. 10173, otherwise known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (DPA) of the Philippines, its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR), and all relevant circulars issued by the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

1. Governance & Entity Classification

  • Personal Information Controller (PIC): NgipinSolution Biñan, with clinic premises located at 028 Santo Tomas Rd, Biñan, Laguna, determines the purposes and clinical means of processing your dental health data.
  • Personal Information Processor (PIP): ClinicFlow acts as the secure technological software platform processing digital scheduling, authentication, and queue records under the direct mandate of the clinic.
  • Designated Data Protection Contact: You may contact our Data Protection Officer regarding privacy concerns at contact@clinicflow.com.

2. Categories of Information Collected

We process only data strictly necessary to deliver quality outpatient dental care, maintain clinical safety, and manage our patient queue:

  • General Personal Information: Full legal name (first, middle, last), biological sex, personal mobile phone number (Philippine 11-digit mobile format), and email address.
  • Sensitive Personal Information (SPI) under Section 13 of RA 10173:
    • Reported medical conditions, cardiovascular history, and systemic ailments.
    • Known drug, latex, and chemical allergies.
    • Clinical dental notes, diagnostic summaries, attending dentist notes, procedure histories, and prescribed medications.
    • Statutory identification details (Senior Citizen ID and PWD ID numbers) presented to claim statutory priority and privileges.
  • Technical & Operational Data: Cryptographic password hashes (passwords are never stored in plain text), session identifiers (`PHPSESSID`), security rate-limiting timestamps, appointment timestamps, and queue movement logs.

3. Lawful Bases for Processing

We process your personal and sensitive health data under sanctioned provisions of the Data Privacy Act of 2012:

  • Explicit Consent (Section 12 & 13(a)): You grant express consent upon creating an account, acknowledging our legal terms, and submitting consultation requests.
  • Medical Diagnosis & Healthcare Treatment (Section 13(e)): Processing is necessary for medical consultation, diagnosis, and healthcare treatment performed by or under the supervision of a licensed dental healthcare professional bound by clinical secrecy.
  • Vital Medical Interests (Section 13(d)): In clinical scenarios where immediate health declarations are necessary to protect life or physical health.
  • Statutory Compliance: Compliance with clinical healthcare recordkeeping standards established by the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC) Board of Dentistry and the Philippine Department of Health (DOH).

4. Purpose Specification

Your records are processed exclusively for legitimate healthcare and administrative purposes:

  • Facilitating appointment scheduling, schedule coordination, and attending doctor calendar allocation.
  • Managing live triage queuing, room assignment, and on-premises audio turn announcements.
  • Furnishing your attending dentist with your prior treatment notes, allergies, and diagnostic history during consultations.
  • Dispatching transactional notifications, appointment confirmations, and password recovery tokens.
  • Fulfilling legal, statutory, and ethical dental recordkeeping mandates.

5. Strict Prohibition on Third-Party Data Sharing

We hold an unwavering stance regarding patient confidentiality: We do not sell, rent, lease, monetize, or disclose your personal or medical records to pharmaceutical advertisers, data brokers, insurance marketers, or external marketing networks.

Disclosures are strictly limited to:

  • Authorized clinic healthcare staff directly participating in your treatment triage.
  • Accredited dental prosthetic laboratories fabricating patient-specific dental appliances (prosthetics, crowns, aligners) under strict non-disclosure obligations.
  • Government or judicial authorities only when formally mandated by a lawful court subpoena, warrant, or statutory public health order.

6. Technical, Organizational & Physical Safeguards

To prevent unauthorized access, accidental loss, unlawful destruction, or alteration of patient data, we enforce multi-tiered safeguards:

  • Cryptographic Protection: User passwords are encrypted using state-of-the-art Bcrypt hashing with randomized salt rounds. Plaintext passwords never touch our database.
  • Database & Transport Security: Web communications are encrypted via SSL/TLS HTTPS. All database transactions are executed strictly using parameterized PHP Data Objects (PDO) prepared statements, completely preventing SQL injection vulnerabilities.
  • Cookie Session Isolation: Authentication cookies are hardened with HttpOnly (blocking JavaScript XSS token theft), SameSite=Lax (neutralizing Cross-Site Request Forgery), and Secure flags over HTTPS.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Administrative, doctor, and staff privileges are strictly isolated. Reception staff cannot view intimate medical diagnosis notes; only verified attending dentists retain medical documentation access.
  • Brute-Force Rate Limiting: Electronic portals enforce automatic account lockouts after 5 consecutive failed login attempts to prevent credential-stuffing attacks.

7. Medical Record Retention & Disposal

  • Healthcare Retention Mandate: In compliance with Philippine healthcare professional standards, clinical malpractice limitation periods, and regulatory guidelines, dental consultation charts, radiographs, and treatment notes are retained for a minimum of 10 to 15 years from the date of the last patient visit.
  • Safe Deletion & Deactivation: When a patient requests account closure, personal portal access is deactivated, and records are safely archived in an ethical soft-deleted state (`is_active = 0`) to preserve clinical audit trails while preventing further electronic access.

8. Your Patient Rights under Republic Act No. 10173

As a data subject under Philippine law, you possess comprehensive rights regarding your information:

  • Right to be Informed: You have the right to know whether personal data pertaining to you is being processed, including the purpose, scope, and recipients of such processing.
  • Right to Access: You may inspect and review your personal profile, past dental visit history, and registered medical conditions directly through your authenticated Patient Portal.
  • Right to Rectification: You have the right to dispute any inaccuracy or error in your personal data and have it corrected immediately via your Portal Settings or clinic administration.
  • Right to Erasure or Blocking: You may request the suspension, withdrawal, or blocking of your personal data upon valid grounds, subject to mandatory statutory healthcare record retention rules.
  • Right to Object: You have the right to object to the processing of your personal data for purposes other than direct healthcare and treatment coordination.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may obtain a copy of your personal data in an electronic, structured, and commonly used format.
  • Right to Damages & NPC Complaint: You have the right to be indemnified for any damages sustained due to inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false, unlawfully obtained, or unauthorized use of personal data, and to lodge a formal complaint with the National Privacy Commission (NPC) at privacy.gov.ph.

Cookie & Technical Storage Policy

The ClinicFlow architecture is engineered with a strict privacy-by-design philosophy. We reject intrusive user profiling, tracking beacons, and cross-site behavioral telemetry. This policy explains what cookies and browser storage tokens are utilized on this platform, why they exist, and how you can manage them.

1. What Are Cookies and Browser Local Storage?

Cookies are small, encrypted text files placed on your device by web servers to maintain authenticated session state. Local Storage is a modern browser feature that allows web applications to store basic, non-sensitive client preferences directly on your local device without sending unnecessary telemetry data to external servers with every request.

ClinicFlow utilizes only first-party, functionally essential technical tokens. We do not load third-party marketing or cross-site advertising trackers:

3. Zero Third-Party Advertising Guarantee

Unlike commercial websites that embed marketing pixels, social media tracking trackers, or ad-retargeting beacons, ClinicFlow contains zero third-party marketing trackers:

  • No Facebook Pixel, Meta Business SDK, or social tracker beacons.
  • No Google Ads remarketing or conversion pixels.
  • No data broker telemetry, behavioral profiling, or cross-site tracking scripts.
  • No third-party content distribution networks tracking your health navigation.

4. Managing & Controlling Browser Cookies

You have full authority to audit, block, or delete cookies through your web browser preferences at any time:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies → Clear browsing data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data.
  • Apple Safari: Settings → Safari → Advanced → Privacy → Block All Cookies.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies.

5. Impact of Disabling Technical Cookies

Because the PHPSESSID cookie is technically essential for server authentication and security, disabling all cookies in your browser settings will prevent you from signing in to your patient portal, booking appointments, or viewing private medical records. The public informational landing page will remain viewable.
