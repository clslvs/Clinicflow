Terms & Conditions of Service

Welcome to ClinicFlow. By creating an account, scheduling an appointment, or using our clinic management platform, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following terms.

1. Clinic Services & Consultations

ClinicFlow provides digital appointment scheduling, queue progression tracking, and medical record management for dental consultations and procedures. Digital confirmations do not replace professional in-person clinical examinations.

2. Patient Responsibilities

  • You agree to provide true, accurate, and complete information regarding your identity, contact details, medical allergies, and existing conditions.
  • You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential. Any activity under your account remains your responsibility.
  • Patients must arrive at least 10 minutes prior to their estimated consultation time or queue call.

3. Appointments & Queue Etiquette

  • Booking & Rescheduling: Pending and confirmed appointments may be rescheduled through your patient portal prior to your appointment time. Completed or in-progress appointments cannot be altered.
  • Queue Progression: Live queue positions and estimated wait times are approximations based on average consultation durations. Urgent or emergency treatments take clinical priority.
  • Cancellations: If you are unable to attend, please cancel your booking promptly through your dashboard so the slot can be released to waiting patients.

4. Priority Patients

In accordance with accessible healthcare standards, Senior Citizens, Persons with Disabilities (PWD), and patients requiring urgent dental intervention are prioritized in our live queue system.

5. Modifications to Service

ClinicFlow reserves the right to modify operating hours, doctor availability schedules, and system terms with reasonable notice posted on the platform.

Privacy Policy & Medical Data Protection

Your privacy and the security of your dental and personal health records are of paramount importance to ClinicFlow. This policy describes how we collect, store, and safeguard your health data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and encrypted password.
  • Dental & Medical Data: Reported allergies, pre-existing health conditions, PWD/senior priority classification, visit history, consultation notes, and doctor treatment plans.
  • Operational Logs: Queue numbers, appointment dates, and visit progression statuses.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To facilitate appointment bookings and manage doctor schedules.
  • To operate the live patient calling system and voice announcement notifications.
  • To provide doctors and authorized clinic staff with your clinical medical history during treatment.
  • To send automated password reset tokens and critical service updates.

3. Confidentiality & Security

All passwords are encrypted with industry-standard bcrypt hashing. Only authenticated clinic personnel (your attending dentist and authorized reception staff) have access to your clinical dental notes. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or medical data to third parties.

4. Your Patient Rights

You have the right to review your personal profile, update your contact and medical allergy details via your Patient Settings, and request an account deactivation at any time by speaking with clinic administration.

Cookie & Local Storage Policy

ClinicFlow is designed to be privacy-friendly. We do not use third-party marketing, ad-tracking, or cross-site profiling cookies.

1. What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies are small data files stored in your browser by websites you visit. Local storage is a browser feature that allows websites to remember simple preferences without transmitting data across external servers.

2. How ClinicFlow Uses Cookies

Because our cookie is strictly necessary for portal authentication and security, disabling cookies in your browser settings will prevent you from logging into your account.
